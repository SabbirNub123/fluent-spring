A New Study Hub for Students

Eastern Delta University opened a new Study Hub in Block C on 3 August 2026. The hub gives students a quiet place to read, write and work in groups.

The hub has 80 seats, 12 computers and two group-study rooms. It is open from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m., Sunday to Thursday. Mr. Rashed Karim, the senior librarian, manages the hub and answers students' questions at the front desk.

Students say the hub is especially useful during exam weeks, when the main library is often full. To book a group-study room, students must show their university ID card at the front desk.

In short, the Study Hub gives students a comfortable and organised space for independent and group learning.

Eastern Delta University is a fictional university created for this lesson.
