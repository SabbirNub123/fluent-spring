Green Campus Day

Haor Valley University will celebrate its first Green Campus Day on Thursday, 3 June 2027. The day aims to encourage students and staff to take better care of the campus environment.

The programme starts at 8:30 a.m. at the Main Gate Field, where volunteers will plant 300 trees. After that, students will join a waste-sorting workshop in Lecture Hall 2. The workshop will be led by Ms. Sabrina Chowdhury, an environmental science lecturer.

The student club Green Hands is organising the event with help from the Registrar's Office. Club president Arif Mahmud says, "We want every student to feel responsible for the campus." Students who want to volunteer should register online by 28 May.

Overall, Green Campus Day is designed to turn small daily actions into lasting habits.
