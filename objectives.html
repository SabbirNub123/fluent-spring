Learning Objectives

Know where you are going before you start. Tick the objectives you already feel confident about, then check again at the end.

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

1. Explain the difference

Explain the difference between skimming and scanning.

2. Find the main idea

Identify the main idea of a reading passage through skimming.

3. Locate details

Locate specific information through scanning.

4. Answer questions

Answer reading comprehension questions using appropriate reading strategies.

The six stages

  1. Warm-up: read a short paragraph and predict.
  2. Delivery and modelling: learn and try both techniques.
  3. Guided practice: follow five steps with hints.
  4. Main task: skimming and scanning challenges.
  5. Review and assessment: 10 questions and a score.
  6. Feedback and revision: improve and reflect.
