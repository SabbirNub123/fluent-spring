Part 1: Skimming (questions 1–5)

Handwriting or Typing: Which Is Better for Lecture Notes?

Many university students now bring laptops to lectures, but some teachers still ask them to write notes by hand. So which method is better? The answer depends on what the student wants to achieve.

Typing is fast. Students can record almost every word the lecturer says and can search their notes later. However, this speed can become a problem, because students may copy sentences without thinking about their meaning.

Handwriting is slower, so students must choose the most important ideas and put them in their own words. This process can help them understand and remember the lesson. On the other hand, handwritten notes are harder to search and to share.

In conclusion, neither method is always best. Students who want a complete record may prefer typing, while those who want deeper understanding may choose handwriting. The best choice is the one that matches the learning goal.
