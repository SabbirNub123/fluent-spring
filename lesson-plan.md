# Lesson Plan – Skimming and Scanning (Group C10)

**Course:** Introduction to ELT (ENG 0231-34) · **Related course:** ENG 0232-05 Reading and Writing Skills
**CLO1:** Summarise and answer questions about academic reading materials.
**Learners:** University students (Bangladesh), approx. CEFR B1–B2 · **Duration:** 60 minutes · **Format:** Classroom or lab, one device per learner (or pair), FluentSpring website
**Materials:** FluentSpring (offline in a browser), projector, optional printed copy of the techniques comparison table.

## Learning objectives
By the end of the lesson, learners can:
1. Explain the difference between skimming and scanning.
2. Identify the main idea of a passage through skimming.
3. Locate specific information through scanning.
4. Answer reading comprehension questions using appropriate reading strategies.

## Stages
| # | Stage (website page) | Time | Teacher does | Learners do | Evidence of participation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Warm-up (`warmup.html`) | 5 min | Introduces the topic, asks "How do you read a notice vs. a textbook chapter?" | Read the Campus Science Fair paragraph; answer 3 questions; read feedback | Warm-up answers and feedback on screen |
| 2 | Delivery, modelling, input (`techniques.html`) | 10 min | Models skimming and scanning aloud using the highlight demos; elicits when each is used | Use the highlight buttons; study the comparison table; complete the "skimming or scanning?" check | Quick-check score |
| 3 | Guided practice (`guided-practice.html`) | 10 min | Circulates; encourages use of hints; pairs weaker and stronger readers | Follow 5 steps (title, first/last sentences, general idea, scan a name, answer questions); use hints | Steps checked 5/5 |
| 4 | Main task (`main-activity.html`) | 15 min | Reminds learners that accuracy matters more than speed; monitors | Activity A: 3 skimming questions. Activity B: 4 scanning short answers on "University Reading Week"; optional timer | Activity A and B scores |
| 5 | Review and assessment (`assessment.html`) | 10 min | Sets individual work; no help with answers | 5 skimming + 5 scanning questions; submit; read score and explanations | Saved score and percentage |
| 6 | Feedback and revision (`feedback.html`) | 10 min | Discusses common errors; asks learners to share one improvement tip | Review mistakes; do the Green Campus Day revision exercise; write reflection | Revision score; written reflection |

## Assessment of learning
- Formative: warm-up, quick check, guided steps, main activity (automatic, rule-based feedback).
- Summative-style: 10-question assessment (scored out of 10).
- Improvement: revision exercise on a comparable passage plus written reflection.

## CLO alignment
CLO1 is met by learners reading academic-style passages, identifying main ideas (summarising skill) and answering questions about them. Stage 2 and 3 build the skills; Stage 4 and 5 apply them; Stage 6 improves them.

## Differentiation and support
- Hints are available on most questions in Guided Practice, Main Activity B and Revision.
- Learners may retry any stage; retries do not penalise them.
- Stronger learners can set the timer in Activity B and compare time and accuracy.
- Weaker readers can work in pairs and read the model answers in Stage 2 aloud.

## Possible problems and responses
| Problem | Response |
|---|---|
| Learners read every word | Reinforce the target: "What exactly am I looking for?" |
| Learners choose a detail as the main idea | Use the Guided Practice step 3 explanation |
| Learners confuse similar dates or numbers | Use the Main Activity B explanations (distractors) |
| No internet | The site works offline; open `index.html` |

## Teacher and learner responsibility
The website checks answers with pre-written rules. The teacher decides pacing, interprets results, and gives final feedback. Learners are responsible for their reflection and next practice choices.
