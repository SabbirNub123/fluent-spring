Master the Art of Skimming and Scanning

Read faster without losing meaning. In this short lesson you will learn two reading techniques, practise them with a university reading passage, and get feedback to improve.

Start Learning See the two techniques

👀 Skimming

Look quickly at a text to get the big picture: what is it mainly about?

🔎 Scanning

Move your eyes over a text to find one exact detail: a date, name or number.

What you will be able to do

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

  1. 1Explain the difference between skimming and scanning.

  3. 3Locate specific information through scanning.

Two techniques, two different jobs

Skimming

Reading a text quickly to understand its general idea or main message.

You read the title, introduction, headings, first sentences and conclusion. You do not read every word.

Scanning

Looking through a text quickly to locate specific information such as names, dates, numbers, places or keywords.

You know what you are looking for, so you ignore everything else.
