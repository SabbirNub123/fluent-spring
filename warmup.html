Campus Science Fair

Every February, students at Padma Riverside University take part in the Campus Science Fair. This year, 120 students from five departments will present projects about clean water, solar energy and recycling. The fair will be held on Thursday, 11 February 2027, in the university gymnasium. Visitors can vote for their favourite project, and the winners will meet local business leaders who may support their ideas.

Padma Riverside University is a fictional university created for this lesson.
